# Cravio — Parent Company: Quqa Group

## Overview
Quqa Group Co. (شركة القوقا التجارية) is the parent company/investor behind Cravio. They are one of the oldest and largest food industry companies in Jordan.

## History Timeline

| Year | Event |
|---|---|
| 1927 | Mohammad Quqa founded first sweet factory in Jaffa, Palestine — first of its kind in the region |
| 1930 | Expanded to diverse products: sugar candy, chocolate |
| 1940 | War forced move to Amman, Jordan; production resumed |
| 1950 | Commodities and food service ingredients trading business established |
| 1960 | FMCG distribution business launched |
| 1970 | Vertical integration — Quqa Printing Co. established for packaging |
| 1980 | Major expansion; first to bring HORECA equipment solutions to the region |
| 1990 | Regional office expansion |
| 2006 | IPO — became public company (FUTR) on Amman Stock Exchange |
| 2010+ | Continued expansion, digital transformation, e-commerce launch |

## Business Divisions
1. **Food Services & Ingredients** — Bakery solutions, chocolate solutions, dairy, ice cream, meat & snacks
2. **HORECA Equipment** — Hotel, restaurant, catering equipment and supplies
3. **FMCG Distribution** — Fast-moving consumer goods
4. **Technical Support & Training** — Certified training courses, product consultation
