# Cravio — Brand Identity

## Brand Name
- **Primary:** Cravio (كرافيو)
- **Variant spellings found:** Craviio (on Facebook), Cravio (Instagram, domain)
- **Pronunciation:** "Cray-vee-oh" / "كرا-فيو"

## Tagline / Slogan
- **Arabic (primary):** "تجربة تُحكى بـ أناقة خالصة"
  - Translation: "An experience told with pure elegance"
- **English (proposed):** "Crave it. Love it. Repeat."
- **Alternative found:** "CRAVIO SWEETS — CAKES & SWEETS"

## Brand Voice & Positioning
- **Tone:** Premium, elegant, luxurious, aspirational
- **Language:** Bilingual — Arabic primary (RTL), English secondary
- **Instagram bio tone:** Playful but refined — uses 💙, "Coming Soon", emojis
- **Key messaging:** "من قلب الشغف… بدأت حكاية حلايانا" (From the heart of passion... our sweets story began)

### Color Psychology
- **Royal blue/dark blue:** Trust, luxury, professionalism, appetite suppression (balanced by warm gold)
- **Gold:** Premium quality, richness, warmth, indulgence
- **Cream:** Softness, bakery warmth, approachability
- **Chocolate brown:** Grounding, connection to desserts

### Typography
| Use | Font | Style |
|---|---|---|
| Arabic text | **Cairo** (Google Font) | 400, 500, 600, 700, 800, 900 |
| English text | **Poppins** (Google Font) | 300, 400, 500, 600, 700 |
| Display/logo | **Playfair Display** (Google Font) | 400, 500, 600, 700, 800 + Italics |

### Logo
- **Verified (via screenshot):** Lowercase "cravio" wordmark with decorative crown element above the "c", white/light on dark background
- **Design (from menu.html mockup):** Circular gold mark with letter "C" in Playfair Display
- **Wordmark:** "CRAVIO" in Playfair Display, letter-spaced
- **Note:** Actual production logo may differ from menu.html mockup — screenshot shows lowercase, not uppercase

### Design System Elements
- **Border radius:** Rounded (16px cards, 50px buttons/pills)
- **Animations:** Fade-in-up on cards, pulse animation on hero badge
- **Background pattern:** Subtle dessert-themed repeating pattern (dots suggesting cake slices, diagonal bakery stripes)
- **Glassmorphism:** Frosted glass headers with backdrop blur (12px)
- **Cards:** Dark blue semi-transparent with gold border highlights on hover
- **Gradients:** Gold shimmer animation on "Bestseller" badges
- **WhatsApp FAB:** Green `#25D366` (standard WhatsApp brand color)

## Brand Archetype
- **Primary:** The Lover (sensual, indulgent, pleasure-focused)
- **Secondary:** The Creator (artisanal, crafted, innovative desserts)

## Brand Keywords
فاخر (luxury), أناقة (elegance), حرفي (artisanal), شغف (passion), تجربة (experience), حكاية (story), لحظات (moments)
