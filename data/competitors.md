## Cravio's Competitive Position

### Strengths
- **Premium positioning** — Distinct from mass-market bakeries (Shaltawi, Suger House)
- **Unique product mix** — Stuffed cookies + premium cakes + donuts + chocolate desserts
- **Parent company backing** — Quqa Group supply chain advantage
- **Arabic-first branding** — Resonates with local market
- **Instagram-first marketing** — Building hype before launch
- **Western-style premium desserts** — Differentiated from traditional oriental sweets

### Weaknesses
- **Pre-launch** — No revenue, no customer base
- **No physical storefront yet** (presumed)
- **Price point** — Higher than local competitors (risk in Sahab market)
- **No delivery platform presence** — Not on Talabat, Careem, etc.

### Opportunities
- **First premium cookie shop in Sahab** — Untapped market
- **Custom cake niche** — Few premium custom cake options in Sahab
- **Instagram-to-order pipeline** — Direct monetization of social following
- **Corporate gifting** — Premium dessert boxes via Quqa's corporate network
- **Delivery platform expansion** — Talabat, Careem, Noon, Keeta

### Threats
- **Economic conditions in Jordan** — Premium pricing may limit market size
- **Sahab demographics** — Industrial/suburban area, may not support luxury dessert pricing
- **Established competitors** — Habibah, Shaltawi have strong local loyalty
- **Copycat risk** — Concept easily replicated
