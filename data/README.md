## Executive Summary

**Cravio** is a premium dessert bakery launching soon in Sahab, Amman, Jordan. It is backed by **Quqa Group Co.** — a publicly-traded food industry giant founded in 1927. The brand positions itself as a luxury dessert experience with a royal blue and gold visual identity, Arabic-forward bilingual branding, and a tagline of elegant indulgence.
