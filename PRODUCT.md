# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

**Primary:** Dessert lovers in Amman and Sahab, Jordan — people who follow food Instagram accounts, seek premium baked goods, and value quality over price. They discover Cravio through Instagram (@cravio.jo, 229 followers and growing), WhatsApp shares, or local search.

**Secondary:** Special-occasion gift buyers — birthdays, graduations, Eid, Ramadan, weddings. These users need custom cake ordering and premium presentation. They may not be Instagram-native but find Cravio through word of mouth or Google.

**Context:** Users are on mobile (Instagram → link-in-bio), hungry, browsing. They want to see what's available, check prices, and order — fast. The page must load instantly on Jordanian mobile networks and feel premium even on a mid-range phone.

## Product Purpose

Cravio is a pre-launch premium dessert bakery. The landing page exists to:

1. **Convert Instagram hype** into a professional, trustworthy digital storefront
2. **Capture pre-launch interest** — email/WhatsApp signups for launch notifications
3. **Showcase the product line** with mouth-watering presentation that drives orders
4. **Drive WhatsApp orders** as the primary conversion action
5. **Establish brand credibility** before the physical storefront opens

Success looks like: a visitor lands from Instagram, sees what Cravio offers, feels the premium quality, and messages on WhatsApp to order — within 30 seconds.

## Brand Personality

**Elegant, warm, indulgent.** The Arabic tagline captures it: "تجربة تُحكى بـ أناقة خالصة" (An experience told with pure elegance).

The brand is luxurious but never cold. It's premium but welcoming — like a high-end Amman patisserie where you feel special, not intimidated. The warmth comes from the product (cookies, cakes, chocolate), not from forced friendliness. The indulgence is in the craftsmanship and ingredients, not in excess or loudness.

**Voice:** Arabic-forward, poetic but not ornate. English is present but secondary. Every word choice signals quality without pretension.

## Anti-references

**This is NOT:**
- A SaaS landing page — no hero metrics, gradient cards, tech-company templates, or "modern startup" energy
- A traditional Arabic sweet shop — no Islamic geometric pattern overload, no gold filigree on white backgrounds, no ornate calligraphy as decoration
- A budget dessert chain — no loud colors, no price-explosion layouts, no "cheap and cheerful" vibe
- A cookie-cutter AI landing page — no numbered section markers, no tiny uppercase eyebrow labels on every section, no glassmorphism, no gradient text

**Reference direction:** High-end Amman cafes and boutiques — spaces where you'd find premium coffee, thoughtful design, and a modern Arab identity that feels authentic, not imported.

## Design Principles

1. **The product is the hero.** Every design decision serves the desserts. Photography, descriptions, and presentation drive desire. Decoration never competes with the cookies.

2. **Arabic-first, not Arabic-as-afterthought.** The typography, layout, reading direction, and cultural cues are native to Arabic. English is accommodated, not centered.

3. **Warmth through restraint.** The brand is warm and indulgent, but the design earns that warmth through material choices (color, type, photography) rather than through decorative excess. Less ornament, more substance.

4. **Instant to desire, instant to order.** Every screen must answer "what do they have?" and "how do I get it?" within seconds. WhatsApp is one tap away, always.

5. **Jordanian premium, not imported luxury.** The design feels at home in Amman. It references local premium culture — modern Arab identity, not French patisserie cosplay or Western bakery templates.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

**Target:** WCAG 2.1 AAA where achievable, AA minimum everywhere.

- **Contrast:** All text meets enhanced contrast ratios (7:1 for body, 4.5:1 for large text)
- **Touch targets:** Minimum 44×44px for all interactive elements (mobile-first market)
- **RTL:** Full Arabic right-to-left support with proper bidirectional text handling
- **Screen readers:** Semantic HTML, ARIA labels, alt text on all product images
- **Reduced motion:** All animations respect `prefers-reduced-motion`
- **Keyboard:** Full keyboard navigation support
- **Mobile networks:** Optimized for Jordanian 3G/4G — fast load, minimal data
